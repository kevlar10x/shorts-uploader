Privacy policy

What Shorts Uploader can see, what it keeps, and how to take its access away.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Who runs it

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool made by kevlar10x that uploads videos to its owner's YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services, so by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it can access

When you sign in, Shorts Uploader asks Google for four permissions: uploading videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload), viewing your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), viewing your channel's YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) and managing your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl), which is the permission Google requires for posting comments. It uses the two viewing permissions only to read statistics for the videos it uploaded: views, likes, comment counts, watch time and how much of each video people watched. It uses the managing permission only to post one comment from your channel under each video it uploaded. It doesn't ask for your name, email address or contacts, it doesn't read your comments, playlists or subscriptions, and it doesn't change or delete anything on your channel.

What it stores

After you sign in, Google gives the tool a token that lets it upload, read those statistics and post those comments. The token is saved on the owner's PC, encrypted with Windows data protection so only that Windows user can read it. The tool also keeps a local list of the videos it has made, the YouTube IDs they were given and their latest statistics. None of this leaves the PC, apart from the videos and their details going to YouTube.

How it's used and shared

The token is only used to upload videos, set their title, description, tags, category, language and privacy, read the statistics above so the owner can see which videos people watch, and post one comment under each uploaded video. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for ads or tracking. The tool shows no ads or third-party content and uses no cookies. This site sets no cookies either; GitHub, which hosts it, keeps its own server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Shorts Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can take away the tool's access at any time on Google's third-party access page. The tool deletes its saved token the next time it runs after that, and it runs every hour while the owner's PC is on. To have the token deleted sooner, email the address below and it will be done within 7 days.

Deleting the tool's data doesn't remove anything from YouTube. Videos it already uploaded stay on your channel until you delete them in YouTube Studio.
