Shorts Uploader

A personal tool that uploads finished videos to its owner's YouTube channel.

How it works

It runs on one Windows PC, with no sign-up and no server behind it. When a video is finished, the tool sends the file with its title, description and tags to YouTube through the YouTube Data API, then marks the video private or schedules it to go public.

What it asks Google for

Permission to upload videos to the YouTube channel you sign in with, and to read the numbers on the videos it uploaded (views, likes, watch time). It doesn't read your comments, playlists, email or anything else in your Google account. The privacy policy explains what the tool stores and how to take its access away.

Read the privacy policy

Who can use it

Only its owner. It isn't offered to anyone else. If a Google sign-in screen sent you here and you weren't expecting it, close that screen and don't give access.
